# Figma recommendation-card sources

These are full-resolution, real production screens captured from the isolated
US App Store UI-test journey. Import them into Figma, mask the phone chrome,
and crop the white recommendation cards as needed.

- `01-add-weight-full.png`: 135 lb × 10 becomes 140 lb × 8.
- `02-add-one-rep-full.png`: keep 95 lb and move from 8 to 9 reps.
- `03-repeat-target-full.png`: repeat 115 lb after missing the 9-rep target.

The three states are designed to be stacked as visual proof that Unit chooses
the smallest understandable next action instead of blindly adding weight.
